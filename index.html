AI Auto-Answer Evaluation — Transit Inquiry

THE DECISION PROBLEM

How should an AI support assistant respond to a reported bus/train delay when no verified service alert is available? This simulated evaluation applies the same customer-service standards used in human response operations: factual grounding, clear next steps, brand voice and escalation.

Sample inquiry: “I am at Atlantic Ave waiting 20 minutes for the Q train. Is it running? No updates anywhere.”

Critical judgment

If an AI response says it checked live status, that check must actually have occurred and be logged. It must not invent a reason for the delay or promise a follow-up time unless an operational team can meet it.

Evaluation decision: Conditional pass only after official status grounding, escalation routing, and SLA are verified. Without those controls, the simulated reply is not production-ready.

Human handoff schema

Line / direction / station • Customer reported wait • Last verified status time • Escalation owner • Next update deadline • Verified response

Zone Reporting — 10 Reports to 3 Executive Tabs

THE DECISION PROBLEM

Monthly regional performance reporting required aggregating 10 separate zone reports, updating Excel and building PowerPoint narratives under an early-month-close deadline. An AI-assisted workflow was designed to speed draft reporting while retaining human control of data quality and interpretation.

Inputs
10 reports
Zone performance sources
Standardized outputs
3 tabs
Summary / Performance / Actions
Publishing goal
BD 5
Business-day-five monthly close

Operating workflow

1. Structured source inputs

Organize production, pipeline, productivity and coaching activity by zone.

2. AI-assisted first draft

Prompt OpenAI tools for consistent zone tables, significant changes and plain-language executive commentary.

3. Excel source of truth

Link three executive tabs with controlled metrics, definitions and formula-based relationships.

4. Human QA & publication

Reconcile commentary to Excel; rebuild PowerPoint from validated figures, then approve distribution.

Exception handling example

Draft: North Zone increased 12% month over month.
Validated calculation: 8%.
Control: Hold publication, reconcile the lagging zone total, regenerate commentary and review again.

Prompt pattern

Summarize each zone's production, pipeline, productivity and coaching using ONLY the supplied values. Flag changes over 10%. Draft three executive bullets. Mark missing source fields; do not invent figures.

Demonstrated competencies: AI workflow design, Excel formula controls, prompt structuring, human QA, executive reporting, reporting-cycle management and change communication. Disclosure: Portfolio description provided by its author; no proprietary employer metrics or documents displayed.

The fairness safeguard

Raw sales totals can privilege larger or longer-tenured groups. A fair comparison requires an aligned denominator and comparable cohorts before interpreting a difference.

Analytic rule: Compare productivity per registered representative, holding tenure bands and definitions consistent. Label small cohorts and avoid causal claims.

AI-assisted design pattern

Using only the supplied Excel metrics, create filterable HTML trends for seven domains and year-over-year comparisons. Flag small cohorts. Never fill missing values or change metric definitions.

Manager data is modeled separately. Changes in a filter recalculate every applicable metric. No data leaves this HTML file, and it works offline.

People Analytics / HR bridge

  • Talent acquisition: applications → screens → interviews → offers → starts.
  • HR operations: conversion by recruiting source, cohort and territory.
  • Retention: early attrition and 12-month survivorship by new-hire cohort.
  • Management: distinct field-manager retention and potential staffing instability.
  • Governance: headcount denominators, small sample flags, definitions and restrictions on sensitive data.

What would make this an enterprise-ready analysis?

  • Confirm official stage definitions, source attribution and duplicate handling.
  • Build HRIS/ATS-to-report mapping and consistent requisition/hire identifiers.
  • Validate denominator integrity and the 12-month measurement window.
  • Consider tenure, market size and manager assignment as confounders.
  • Require privacy/HR approval for protected-class and individual-level analysis.
